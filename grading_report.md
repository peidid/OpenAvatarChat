## HANDS-ON REVISION STRATEGIES

### Strategy 1: Sentence-Level Revision Exercise

1. **Identify long sentences**: Find all sentences over 35 words
2. **Ask**: Does this sentence contain 2+ distinct ideas?
3. **If yes**: Split into two sentences, ensuring each has a clear subject-verb-object structure
4. **Test**: Read each sentence aloud. If you need to pause for breath, it's probably too long.

### Strategy 2: Synthesis Enhancement Exercise

1. **Create a synthesis map**: Draw connections between authors on paper
2. **For each connection, ask**: "How does Author A's finding relate to Author B's finding?"
3. **Add explicit connection phrases**: 
   - "Building on X's findings..."
   - "Similarly, Y demonstrates..."
   - "While X focuses on..., Y extends this by..."
   - "These findings converge to show..."

### Strategy 3: Evidence Integration Check

For each claim, verify:
- [ ] Is there a citation?
- [ ] Does the evidence directly support the claim?
- [ ] Is there a sentence explaining HOW the evidence supports the claim?
- [ ] Is the evidence integrated smoothly (not dropped in)?

### Strategy 4: Academic Style Refinement

1. **Read aloud**: This helps identify awkward phrasing
2. **Use the "so what?" test**: After each paragraph, ask "So what?" If you can't answer clearly, the paragraph needs strengthening
3. **Vary sentence structure**: Mix simple, compound, and complex sentences
4. **Precision check**: Replace vague words with specific ones
